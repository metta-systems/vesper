Engine code (room::engine, room::types) that needs more than
core + alloc, i.e. what stops the engine building for Vesper.

Nothing. The engine library is #![no_std] + alloc outside #[cfg(test)]
(src/lib.rs) and reaches the OS only through the Platform trait
(src/engine/platform.rs): files (WAD handles and whole-file helpers),
quit and error_box. It logs through the `log` crate and allocates through
`alloc` (the zone heap with alloc::alloc), served by each binary's
#[global_allocator]. It has no extern "C" declarations and does not
depend on the libc crate. `cargo check --lib --target
aarch64-unknown-none-softfloat` proves it; std is used only by unit tests,
tests/ and the binary's platforms in src/platform/.
